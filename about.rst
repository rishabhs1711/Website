Hi!
I am a 1st year PhD student at Universität Regensburg under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Guido Kings. 
I am interested in Number Theory and Arithmetic Geometry, more specifically CM abelian varieties, :math:`L`-functions, Kronecker-Eisenstein Series and the interplay thereof. 

I did my Master's in the `ALGANT <https://algant.eu/>`_ program, spending my first year at Universiteit Leiden and the second at Universität Regensburg. My Master's thesis was about explicit class field theory in the CM field case, a copy can be found `here </pdfs/thesis.pdf>`_.

Before that I was at the `Chennai Mathematical Institute <https://www.cmi.ac.in/>`_ for my bachelor's.
