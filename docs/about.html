About

Hi! I am a 1st year PhD student at Universität Regensburg under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Guido Kings. I am interested in Number Theory and Arithmetic Geometry, more specifically CM abelian varieties, L-functions, Kronecker-Eisenstein Series and the interplay thereof.

I did my Master’s in the ALGANT program, spending my first year at Universiteit Leiden and the second at Universität Regensburg. My Master’s thesis was about explicit class field theory in the CM field case, a copy can be found here.

Before that I was at the Chennai Mathematical Institute for my bachelor’s.
